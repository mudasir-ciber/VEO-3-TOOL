How Chained Evolution Studio Works

🔒 The Ironclad Chain Rule:

1. Scene 1 uses your Master Image as the initial visual reference.

2. The extension submits Scene 1's prompt to Google Flow and waits until video generation completes 100%.

3. The extension retrieves the generated MP4 and extracts the exact last frame as a crisp PNG image.

4. Scene 2 automatically uploads that extracted last frame as its reference. The Master Image is never used for Scene 2 or later!

5. The cycle repeats continuously across all scenes in your batch.
